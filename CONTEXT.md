# SURGE Website

The SURGE website introduces the product system and invites people to request early access.

## Language

**Shareable preview**:
A public-by-link, non-indexed review build that demonstrates the website and permits only the owner's end-to-end test signup. It does not accept public enrollment.
_Avoid_: Launch site, private build, live public signup

**Test signup**:
An owner-only exercise of the complete email submission and confirmation journey. It verifies the system without representing public enrollment.
_Avoid_: Public signup, confirmed subscriber

**Launch site**:
The public SURGE website that can enroll visitors in the early-access list.
_Avoid_: Shareable preview, prototype

**Early-access list**:
The list of confirmed subscribers who want to know when the first SURGE products become available.
_Avoid_: Newsletter, mailing list

**Pending signup**:
An email submission awaiting address confirmation. It is not yet part of the early-access list.
_Avoid_: Subscriber, early-access member

**Confirmed subscriber**:
A person who confirmed control of their email address and joined the early-access list.
_Avoid_: Pending signup, unverified contact

**Unsubscribed contact**:
A former confirmed subscriber who no longer permits marketing email. The person is not part of the early-access list, but a minimal suppression record remains so the preference is honored.
_Avoid_: Confirmed subscriber, deleted contact

**Suppression record**:
The minimum record retained solely to prevent marketing to an address that opted out.
_Avoid_: Subscriber profile, active contact

**Deletion request**:
A person's request to remove their signup and consent information. It is distinct from unsubscribing because a minimal suppression record may still be required to honor the opt-out.
_Avoid_: Unsubscribe, automatic contact deletion

**Marketing consent**:
A confirmed subscriber's specific permission to receive SURGE early-access and product-launch emails, recorded only after double opt-in.
_Avoid_: Email submission, implied consent

**Neutral signup response**:
The same public check-your-inbox response for any valid email, regardless of whether it is new, pending, or already confirmed.
_Avoid_: Subscriber lookup result, account-status response
