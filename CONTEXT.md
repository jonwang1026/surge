# SURGE Website

The SURGE website introduces the product system and invites people to request early access.

## Language

**Frontend preview**:
A private review build that demonstrates the website’s design and interactions without enrolling visitors in early access.
_Avoid_: Live signup, production site

**Launch site**:
The public SURGE website that can enroll visitors in the early-access list.
_Avoid_: Frontend preview, prototype

**Early-access list**:
The list of people who want to know when the first SURGE products become available.
_Avoid_: Newsletter, mailing list
